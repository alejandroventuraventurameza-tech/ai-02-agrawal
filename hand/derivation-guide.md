# Authentic handwritten proof: required student evidence

The issue requires at least **one genuine photograph** of your own
handwritten derivation. It cannot be replaced by AI-generated handwriting,
typeset mathematics, or this guide. Name the uploaded image
`hand/prop3-derivation.jpg` (or .png) and link it from README.

For a compact one-page proof, work out these steps yourself:

1. Start from \(\max_{e\ge0}\alpha\Delta\sqrt{se+\theta}-e\).
   Differentiate the interior objective and obtain
   \(e_{\rm FOC}=\alpha^2\Delta^2s/4-\theta/s\).
2. Record the necessary interior domain
   \(\theta<\alpha^2\Delta^2s^2/4\).
3. Substitute the FOC and write
   \(V=\Gamma(\alpha^2\Delta^2s/4+\theta/s)\).
4. Differentiate the cross-sectional variance and explain why
   \(a_0<0\) does **not** establish \(\operatorname{Var}'(1)>0\):
   the extra inequality \(\theta^*<1\) is required.
5. Audit the **specific recorded AI answer** in `prompts.md`:
   with \(\alpha=1,\Delta=4\), \(p^*=\alpha\Delta s/2=2s\).
   For \(s\in\{3/5,1\}\), both values \(6/5\) and \(2\)
   exceed one. Circle the missing probability check and the verdict:
   *algebraically correct, not an admissible literal probability example*.
6. In a small final note, distinguish this audit of the original square-root
   index from our different *capped* probability extension in
   `extra/bounded-probability-variant.md`.

Photograph the complete page with legible symbols and the final conclusion.
It is fine to show corrections or crossed-out steps. Do not add a fictional
date or claim it was written by someone else. When the real image has been
supplied, the Beamer workflow automatically inserts it on slide 4.
