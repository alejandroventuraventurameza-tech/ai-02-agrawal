Bicycles for the Mind*. NBER Working Paper 34034, 53 pages.

- Paper page: https://www.nber.org/papers/w34034
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3386/w34034
- Direct PDF: https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w34034/w34034.pdf

The paper is an NBER working paper circulated for discussion and comment. It has
not undergone peer review or the review that accompanies official NBER
publications.
